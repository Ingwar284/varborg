# Untyped damage review (GAPS 313)

Every power, spell and foe gloom ability that dealt damage of no type, and the type it now has (17 November 2026). Change any you disagree with on the item in Foundry, or tell Claude, and the compendium packs are rebuilt.

How the type was chosen, where the text names no element: a Sanity test is mental; a grab, slam, charge, trample, fall or buffet is bludgeoning; a bite, sting, claw-grip, spike or thrown blade is piercing; a cut is slashing; gas, spores, smoke and miasma are poison; darkness and the Silence are dark; a blinding flash is light; raw magic with no element is force. A power whose damage only adds to a weapon attack keeps the weapon's type and stays untyped.
