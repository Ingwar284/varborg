# Damage types review (GAPS 289)

Every item with a damage type: its old type and the new one. Change any you disagree with on the item in Foundry, or tell Claude, and the compendium packs are rebuilt.

The Table of Agony column follows from the new type: bludgeoning Blunt force, slashing Bleeding, piercing (Gun)shot, fire Fire & explosives, and piercing or slashing with a d10 or bigger die Gore & massive.
