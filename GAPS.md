# GAPS

Rules that were missing or unclear for the system to apply, and the
designer's ruling. The rules document (_reference/AI_BRIDGE_-_VÅR_BORG.md)
stays the authority; these rulings fill its gaps.

Status: **Resolved** = ruled by the designer. **Open** = still needs a ruling.

## Open questions

| # | Question | Ruling | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 55 | Lockpicks have two expendable dice by test result (Failure d6, Ruin d2), but an item holds one expendable die. How should the system handle that? | Deferred until tests can be tied to items. Until then lockpicks have no expendable die in the system and are rolled by hand. | Open |

## Combat

| # | Question | Ruling | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | Attack Triumph reduces the foe's "armour tier", but foes use an armour die. | Step the armour die down one (d8 → d6 → d4 → d2 → none). d2 = t1, d4 = t2, d6 = t3, d8 = t4. | Resolved |
| 13 | DR of the Toughness test when gaining a wound? | DR12. The Dark Master may change it (for example for grotesque wounds). | Resolved |
| 14 | The Table of Agony rows look broken. | Merged tables. Severity bands: 1 flesh wound, 2–5 minor injury, 6–7 major injury, 8–9 lethal injury, 10 fatal injury. Rows without a severity cell read as: d10, Blunt force, Bleeding, (Gun)shot, Fire & explosives, Gore & massive. | Resolved |
| 15 | Weapons have no damage type for the Agony column. | The Dark Master chooses. The system suggests the obvious type for each weapon. | Resolved |
| 16 | Death's Door row 2: what counts as a feat? | Only feats gained through Improving. | Resolved |
| 17 | Attack Ruin: "spend an action to recover". | Add a Recover button. | Resolved |
| 18 | Do reactions count towards the multiple-attack Bane? | Only when the reaction happens on the PC's own turn. | Resolved |
| 19 | Persistent damage from several sources? | Exception to "Multiple Effects": different types all apply. Of the same type, only the strongest applies. Each one has its own Toughness test to end it. | Resolved |
| 20 | Is there a maximum Fatigued level? | No. | Resolved |
| 21 | Carrying more than twice the heavy-item (cumbersome) limit "is the death of you". | Changed: movement becomes 0. | Resolved |
| 48 | What counts as a heavy item for carrying, and is it the weapon trait Heavy? | The system calls the rules' "heavy items" **cumbersome**, so they are not confused with the weapon trait Heavy (+2 DR) or heavy shields. Every carried item has a separate "cumbersome" box. It starts ticked for weapons with the Heavy or Two-handed trait, heavy shields and armour of tier 1 and up, and the Dark Master can untick it. Armour you are wearing never counts. Encumbered does not lower movement (all terrain counts as difficult instead). | Resolved |
| 36 | Death's Door 9–10 says "a random body part destroyed", but there is no body-part table. | Roll d10: 1 right hand, 2 left hand, 3 right arm, 4 left arm, 5 right leg, 6 left leg, 7 right foot, 8 left foot, 9 right eye, 10 left eye. The Dark Master decides what the loss does (question 42). | Resolved |
| 37 | Persistent damage of the same type keeps "the strongest". How do we compare dice such as 2d4 and d6? | The one with the highest average damage (2d4 = 5 beats d6 = 3.5). | Resolved |
| 38 | Initiative ties go to the players. What about a tie between two PCs, or two foes? | Decided at random. | Resolved |
| 42 | What does losing a body part do in play? | The Dark Master decides. The system only records which part was lost. | Resolved |
| 39 | Ammunition rolls its expendable die after a combat in which it was used. Automatic or by hand? | When combat ends, the system asks automatically for each ranged weapon fired during that combat. | Resolved |
| 46 | Can a character have more than one armour, or more than one shield, equipped at once? | Yes, any number can be equipped, but only the strongest effect applies: the armour with the highest current tier counts (soak die, DR penalties, metal), and a heavy shield beats a light one. | Resolved |
| 49 | The DR table runs 6 to 26. Can armour push a DR above 26 (for example DR 22 with special armour, +6)? | No. A modified DR is capped at 26. | Resolved |
| 50 | Two equipped armours with the same current tier but different original tiers (one damaged): which one's Agility penalty counts? | The one with the highest original tier. | Resolved |
| 51 | Does armour's "+DR on Agility tests" change a contested Agility test, which has no DR? | Yes, it applies to all Agility tests. In a contested test, which has no DR, the penalty is taken off the character's total instead (+4 DR becomes −4). | Resolved |
| 47 | Weapon groups (Weapon specialist feat): is there a fixed list? | No. Weapon groups are flavour, not a defined game term, so weapons don't store one. | Resolved |
| 52 | When an expendable die rolls a 1, is the item deleted or kept? | Kept and marked **used up** (for a weapon: **out of ammunition**). Its Expendable button disappears. A weapon out of ammunition cannot be reloaded; a shot already loaded can still be fired. Unticking the mark refills it, for example after buying a new supply (rules 4.1, Ammunition). | Resolved |
| 53 | Does a weapon with Reload start loaded? | Yes. Firing empties it; reloading costs its listed Interact actions and loads it. | Resolved |
| 54 | Items bought or found in numbers (Sanity + 4 torches, 10 iron nails, dried food per day): should gear have a quantity, or is each one a separate item? | Gear has a quantity; weapons, armour and shields do not. Each piece counts: a stack of 3 cumbersome barrels is 3 cumbersome items, and 6 torches fill 6 places in a container. On an expendable 1, one of the stack is used up (quantity −1); the last one is marked used up. A use that is not a 1 (drinking one potion) is lowered by hand. | Resolved |
| 44 | Can current HP go above max HP? | No. HP is capped at max HP, and lowering max HP lowers current HP to match. | Resolved |
